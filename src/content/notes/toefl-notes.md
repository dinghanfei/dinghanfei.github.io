### 1. Urban Life

#### 1. 居住理由： Why do you like living in a city?

**100 words**

I currently live in a large city, and it suits me well, especially as a student. First, city life makes my daily routine convenient. Public transportation, libraries, supermarkets, and restaurants are closely connected, so I can handle study and daily needs without wasting time. For example, on a busy school day, I can go to class, buy something I need, and get back to campus quickly. Second, the city exposes me to more opportunities. I can attend lectures, visit museums, or meet people from different backgrounds. Overall, city life is busy, but it helps me live efficiently and keep growing.

---

#### 2. 城市与小镇对比： Big city or small town?

**99 words**

I would prefer to live in a big city, at least at this stage of my life. The main reason is opportunity. A big city gives students better access to internships, workshops, and cultural events, which help us explore future choices. For example, I may attend a technology talk or meet people who work in my field. Also, city life is efficient because schools, hospitals, and transportation are usually well-connected. I can finish many tasks in one day. A small town may be quieter, but for a student who wants to grow, a big city is more useful.

---

#### 3. 公共空间与社区： Should cities build more public spaces?

**97 words**

Yes, I think cities should build more public spaces, especially parks and community centers. First, they improve people’s physical and mental health. Many residents spend most of the day indoors, so a nearby park gives them a place to walk, exercise, and release stress. For example, after dinner, people can take a short walk instead of staying at home. Second, public spaces create a stronger sense of community. Children can play together, older people can exercise together, and neighbors can talk more often. These spaces are not just decorations; they make city life healthier and more human.

---

#### 4. 城市生活质量： How can cities improve the quality of life?

**100 words**

I think cities can improve the quality of life by making public services more efficient and the environment more livable. First, reliable public services reduce daily stress. For example, if buses, subways, and appointment systems work well, people can save time instead of waiting in long lines. This is important for students and office workers, because their schedules are usually tight. Second, cities should add more green spaces and walkable streets. They give people a place to relax after work or study and make the city feel less crowded. In my opinion, a good city should be efficient, but also comfortable.

---

### 2. Commuting & Transportation

#### 1. 靠近通勤点居住

**Question:** Would you prefer to live close to your school or workplace? Why?

**核心理由：效率 + 健康**

I would prefer to live close to my school or workplace, even if the rent is a little higher. The biggest reason is efficiency. A shorter commute means I can spend less time on the road and more time studying, resting, or doing things I enjoy. For example, if I live near campus, I can go back to my dorm between classes instead of waiting outside. Another reason is health. Long commutes are tiring and stressful, especially during rush hour. So living near the place I go to every day would improve both my schedule and quality of life.

---

#### 2. 汽车与公交对比

**Question:** Would you rather drive a car or take public transportation? Why?

**核心理由：经济 + 环保**

I would choose public transportation over driving a car, especially in a large city. First, it is more economical. Owning a car means paying for gas, parking, insurance, and maintenance, while taking the subway or bus is usually much cheaper. For example, as a student, I would rather save that money for books or daily expenses. Second, public transportation is better for the environment. If more people take buses or subways, there will be fewer cars on the road and less air pollution. Driving may be more private, but for daily commuting, public transportation is more practical.

---

#### 3. 通勤压力

**Question:** Why do many people feel stressed during their daily commute? How can this problem be reduced?

**核心理由：效率 + 健康**

I think commuting stress mainly comes from wasted time and crowded spaces. When people are stuck in traffic or packed into a subway, they may feel tired before the day even starts. For example, a long morning commute can make students or office workers lose energy and patience. To reduce this problem, cities should make public transportation more reliable, so people do not have to wait too long. Individuals can also use commuting time better, like listening to podcasts or reviewing notes. In this way, commuting can become less stressful.

---

#### 4. 未来通勤模式

**Question:** How do you think people will commute in the future?

**核心理由：效率 + 环保**

I think future commuting will become smarter and greener. First, technology will make transportation more efficient. For example, apps can help people choose the fastest route, avoid traffic, and combine different options like subway and shared bikes. This saves time and reduces frustration. Second, commuting will become more environmentally friendly. More people may use electric buses, shared bikes, or work from home part of the week, which can reduce pollution and traffic pressure. Overall, future commuting should be faster, cleaner, and more flexible.

---

#### 1. 热门职业 Popular Jobs

**Question:** What kind of job do you think will be popular in the future? Why?

**核心理由：效率 + 成就 / 经验**

I think technology-related jobs will become very popular in the future, especially jobs connected to artificial intelligence and data analysis. First, these jobs can improve efficiency in many industries. For example, AI engineers can help hospitals analyze medical information faster or help companies make better decisions. Second, these jobs give people a strong sense of achievement because they solve real problems. If someone builds a useful tool that saves time for many people, their work can have a clear impact. So I believe technology jobs will be attractive because they are useful, challenging, and meaningful.

---

#### 2. 满意度与薪资 Job Satisfaction or Salary

**Question:** Which is more important when choosing a job, salary, or job satisfaction?

**核心理由：经济 + 情感 / 健康**

I think both are important, but job satisfaction matters more in the long run. Of course, a salary is necessary because people need money to support themselves and their families. A good income can reduce financial pressure and make life more stable. However, if a person dislikes their job, they may feel stressed every day, even with a high salary. For example, someone who enjoys teaching may feel happier as a teacher than in a better-paid but boring office job. So I believe salary is important, but satisfaction is what keeps people motivated and healthy.

---

#### 3. 一生中的职业转变 Career Change

**Question:** Do you think it is normal for people to change careers during their lives? Why?

**核心理由：经验 + 成就 / 适应变化**

Yes, I think it is normal for people to change careers during their lives. First, people’s interests and goals may change as they gain more experience. For example, a person may start as an engineer but later realize that they enjoy management or teaching more. Second, society is changing quickly, so people need to adapt to new opportunities. Learning new skills and entering a new field can be challenging, but it can also bring a stronger sense of achievement. Overall, changing careers is not a failure; it can be a way to grow.

---

#### 4. AI对未来工作的影响 AI and Future Work

**Question:** How will AI influence people’s jobs in the future?

**核心理由：效率 + 经验 / 成就**

I think AI will change many jobs, but it will not simply replace all workers. First, AI can improve efficiency by handling repetitive tasks. For example, it can help people organize documents, analyze data, or answer basic customer questions. This allows workers to focus on more creative or complex problems. Second, people will need to learn new skills and work with technology instead of avoiding it. For students, this means we should develop both technical skills and communication skills. Overall, AI will make some jobs disappear, but it will also create new opportunities.

### 4. Work–Life Balance

#### 1. 个人平衡策略 Personal Balance Strategy

**Question:** How do you balance your study or work with your personal life?

**核心理由：效率 + 健康**

**约96 words**

I try to balance my study and personal life by making a clear schedule and protecting some time for rest. First, planning helps me become more efficient. For example, I usually decide what tasks are most important and finish them before doing less urgent things. This helps me avoid wasting time. Second, I think rest is necessary for health and long-term productivity. If I study for too many hours without a break, I may feel tired and lose focus. So I try to exercise, talk with friends, or simply relax. A good balance helps me work better.

---

#### 2. 公司支持项目 Company Support

**Question:** What can companies do to help employees achieve work-life balance?

**核心理由：健康 + 效率 / 情感**

I think companies can help employees achieve work-life balance by offering flexible schedules and creating a healthier work culture. First, flexible working hours can reduce stress. For example, if employees can avoid rush hour or adjust their schedule when they have family responsibilities, they may feel more comfortable and focused. Second, companies should not encourage unnecessary overtime. When workers have enough time to rest, exercise, and stay with their families, they are more likely to stay motivated. In the long run, supporting work-life balance is not only good for employees but also good for the company.

---

#### 3. 灵活/远程办公 Flexible or Remote Work

**Question:** Do you think remote work is a good thing? Why or why not?

**核心理由：方便 / 效率 + 交流**

I think remote work is useful, but it should be used in a balanced way. The main advantage is convenience. People can save commuting time and work in a more comfortable environment. For example, instead of spending one hour on the road, they can use that time to finish tasks or rest. However, remote work may also reduce communication. Some problems are easier to solve when people discuss them face-to-face, especially in team projects. So I think remote work is a good option, but it should be combined with regular in-person communication.

---

#### 4. 未来态度 Future Attitude

**Question:** Do you think people will care more about work-life balance in the future?

**核心理由：健康 + 情感 / 成就**

Yes, I think people will care more about work-life balance in the future. First, people are becoming more aware of mental and physical health. A high salary is important, but if someone is always tired or stressed, their quality of life will be poor. For example, many young people now prefer jobs that give them enough personal time. Second, people want a more meaningful life, not just career success. They also want time for family, hobbies, and personal growth. So in the future, a good job will be judged not only by income, but also by balance.

### 类型1：请求 / 资讯类

这类题的核心是：你想获得某个信息、材料、机会或帮助。

常见场景：

- 选课：询问课程要求、时间、名额、是否适合自己；
- 请求讲义 / 文献：向老师或讲座组织者要材料；
- 询问活动信息：时间、地点、报名方式、费用；
- 咨询项目：申请条件、截止日期、所需材料；
- 请求预约： office hour、meeting、advising appointment。

#### 写作目的

问候之后先接一个写作目的：

万能：

1. I am writing to ask for more information about …
2. I would like to ask for more details about…

一些可能的使用场景：

选课、活动、项目、讲座、申请等场景中表达兴趣 + 请求信息：

- I am interested in ..., and I would like to learn more about it.

请求材料：

- I checked the website, but I could not find all the information I needed.
- I am writing to ask if you could share the materials from …

#### 结尾

此外，这类题目可以在最后通过表达感谢来close the message:

万能：

- Thank you very much for your time and help. I look forward to hearing from you.

感谢对方提供信息：

- I would appreciate any information you can provide.  I look forward to hearing from you.

感谢对方发材料：

- I would really appreciate it if you could send me the materials when you have time. I look forward to hearing from you.

选课、申请、是否适合参加某项目等场景，需要对方给建议：

- I would appreciate any advice you could give me. I look forward to hearing from you.

请求预约、申请某活动、询问资格等场景中表达愿意补充信息：

- Please let me know if you need any further information from me.
